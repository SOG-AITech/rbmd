# LJ-salt data package

- RBMD production parameters represented here use cutoff/core `8/4`, RBL
  sample count `30`, and RBE sample count `200`.
- The final workbook is `workbook/run_LJ_salt.xlsx`.
- RBMD scaling and linear-complexity campaigns are under `scaling/rbmd` and
  `linear/rbmd`.
- RDF inputs, scripts, logs, and final CSVs are under `rdf`.
- Matched LAMMPS-Kokkos cutoff `8/8` PPPM references are under
  `lammps_reference`. No LJ cutoff `5/5` campaign is included.
